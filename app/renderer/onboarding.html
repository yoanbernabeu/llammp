Two permissions to grant

Llammp drives Music.app and listens to system audio. macOS requires your consent for both, and neither can be worked around.

! Automation → Music

Lets Llammp read what is playing and control playback. Without it the window stays empty and the transport buttons do nothing.

! Screen & System Audio Recording

Feeds the visualizer with the sound actually coming out of your Mac. Llammp never records your screen — the video track is stopped immediately.
